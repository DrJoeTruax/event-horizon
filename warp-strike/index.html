Warp Strike

A ship the size of a black hole, in a warp bubble, flying at it faster than light. Every pixel’s light is traced through the warped spacetime.

Real physics

  • The warp bubble is the Alcubierre metric, the best-known warp solution of Einstein’s equations: space is squeezed ahead of the bubble and stretched behind it, while the ship inside sits in flat space.
  • The black hole is the Schwarzschild solution, written as space flowing inward (the Painlevé–Gullstrand form), the same black hole as in Event Horizon.
  • Every pixel’s light is traced backward in time through both, while the bubble moves. Nothing about the images is animated by hand: the ship appearing out of nowhere as two images, the backward-flying twin, the fishbowl lens and the color shifts all come from the traced light.
  • Colors and brightness come from each photon’s energy when it left versus when it arrives, so the bubble’s own shifts, the disk’s Doppler shift and gravity’s redshift all show up together.

Imagined

  • A warp bubble needs negative energy, which isn’t known to exist.
  • No one knows the spacetime of a warp bubble and a black hole together. This one adds their two flows of space, a common sketch that is not itself a solution of Einstein’s equations.
  • The burst when the bubble stops is a simplified stand-in for a published result: light and particles piled up on the bubble’s front wall are released ahead of it.
  • The ship and its lights are invented, and the bubble’s interior ignores the black hole’s pull.
