What’s computed

Light paths through the Schwarzschild and Kerr spacetimes, integrated per pixel every frame. The disk’s color is a blackbody temperature, shifted by gravity and by the gas’s own speed.

In The Recomposition, every piece of the ship falls on its own exact orbit. Warp Strike traces light through an Alcubierre bubble laid over the black hole.

What isn’t

Nothing comes out of a real black hole, and no known matter can make a warp bubble. Those stories run on invented premises, and each simulator has a “What’s real” panel that says where the line is.

Needs WebGL2. A dedicated GPU makes it smooth; integrated graphics lowers the resolution to keep up.
